Your questions

How does an NFC Google review card work?+

Your customer holds their phone near the card and your Google “Write a review” page opens right away — no app to install. A QR code printed on the card covers phones with NFC turned off.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?+

Yes. Every iPhone since the XR and the vast majority of Android phones read NFC with no app. The QR code covers the rest.

Can I change where the card leads?+

Yes — for life, without touching the card. The chip points to a Tapora address and we handle the redirect. Google reviews today, your menu or Instagram tomorrow.

What stats do I get?+

Every tap, live: by day, by hour, by weekday, chip or QR, iPhone or Android — with the number of Google reviews gained right beside it. Each business gets a private page and a report on the 1st of every month.

Do you collect data about my customers?+

No. In line with Québec’s Law 25, we keep no IP address, no lasting cookie and no location — only the time of the tap, the channel (chip or QR) and the type of phone.

Where are Tapora cards made?+

In Québec. Tapora S.E.N.C. is a Québec business (NEQ 3382462367), with friendly support in English and French.

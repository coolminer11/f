Your questions

How does an NFC Google review card work?+

Your customer holds their phone near the card and your Google “Write a review” page opens right away — no app to install. Nothing to plug in: the card has no battery.

How much does a Tapora card cost?+

Individual cards are $35 each, with no minimum (5 cards: $175): bilingual and compliant with Québec’s language law — French first in Québec, English first everywhere else. From 25 cards, the Business offer is $30 per card with a custom design. Everything is included, no subscription: live stats, private page, notifications, counter mode, monthly report.

Do you have an offer for businesses (hotels, chains…)?+

Yes: the Business offer, $30 per card from 25 cards. The design is made in your colours (logo, text, a name for each card such as “Room 101”), in a Québec version (French first) or an outside-Québec version (English first). You follow your stats card by card and by group, on a page that can be password-protected. Lead time is longer than for a standard card: a mock-up to approve, then custom production; we confirm the exact date with your mock-up. Send a request

Is there a subscription or a monthly fee?+

No. You pay for the card once and everything else is included, with no monthly fees: stats, private page, “Ding!” notifications, counter mode, review tracking and the monthly report.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?+

Yes. Every iPhone since the XR and the vast majority of Android phones read NFC with no app. On Android it’s the same NFC used for tap-to-pay, so it’s on for most phones.

Can I change where the card leads?+

Yes — any time, without touching the card. The chip points to a Tapora address and we handle the redirect. Google reviews today, your menu or Instagram tomorrow.

What stats do I get?+

Every tap, live: by day, by hour, by weekday, iPhone or Android — with the number of Google reviews gained right beside it. Each business gets a private page and a report on the 1st of every month.

Do you collect data about my customers?+

No. In line with Québec’s Law 25, we keep no IP address, no lasting cookie and no location — only the time of the tap and the type of phone.

Is Tapora a local business?+

Yes. Tapora S.E.N.C. is a Québec business (NEQ 3382462367). Your cards are set up, tracked and supported by us, in Québec, in English and French.

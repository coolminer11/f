Privacy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

When someone taps a Tapora card

We record three things, and only three: the time of the tap, the channel (NFC chip or QR code) and the type of phone (iPhone, Android or other). That’s what powers the business’s statistics.

We keep no IP address, no identifier, no location and no lasting cookie. A 60-second technical cookie only prevents counting the same tap twice; it contains no identifier and expires on its own.

Link previews (messaging apps, social networks) and bots are redirected without being counted.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Fonts and scripts are served from our own domain.

Business customers

For business customers, we keep what the service and billing require: business name, billing details, and an email for the monthly report if requested. Every monthly report includes a way to stop receiving it.

Referrals and Partners page

When a business customer recommends another business to us, we receive what they share: the business name, a contact person, an email or phone number, and an optional note. We use this information only to contact that business once to introduce Tapora, mentioning who recommended us. If they are not interested, we do not follow up; they can ask at any time for this information to be deleted. A business appears on our Partners page (name, website, Google rating and review count) only with its consent, and can be removed on request.

Hosting

Card redirects are served by Cloudflare; data is stored in a Supabase database and our management app is hosted by Render. These providers act on our behalf.

Do the math over two years

A subscription looks small each month, but it adds up. A purely arithmetic example: a $35 card plus a $15 monthly subscription comes to $395 after two years (35 + 24 × 15). With a one-time $35 payment, you stay at $35.

A subscription can make sense if it truly brings more — but you should know before you buy, and compare.

Six questions to ask before buying

  1. What happens if I stop paying? Does the card keep working, or does it stop redirecting?
  2. Can I change the destination myself, or on request, without reprogramming the card?
  3. What exactly will I see? Tap counts? By day, by hour? Reviews gained?
  4. What data is collected about my customers? In Québec, Law 25 governs personal information: the less, the better.
  5. Is there support in my language, with someone who answers?
  6. What’s the total price for the number of cards I need? Register, tables, reception: you often want more than one.

Security

Your data and your customers’ data, protected.

Everything below works today.

  • Password-protected private pagesThe stats page and the staff portal lock with a password. It is hashed (scrypt): no one, not even Tapora, can read it.
  • Signed, renewable linksEvery private link is signed: impossible to guess or forge. A new link makes the old one stop working instantly.
  • Limited attemptsPassword attempts are limited, per device and per hour, and Tapora is alerted to repeated attempts.
  • A separate password for the managerChanging the menu, prices or announcements takes the manager’s password, which expires after 12 hours on each device.
  • No personal data in the statsNo IP address, no identifier: only the time, the channel (NFC or QR code) and the device type.
  • Automatic deletionOn the service pages (menus, requests, concierge): the anti-abuse trace is deleted after 30 days, visits after 13 months, requests and reviews after 25 months.
  • Hosted in Canada, encrypted everywhereData is hosted in Canada. Everything goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and private pages are never indexed by search engines.
  • Protected from abuseRequests are capped, order prices are always recalculated by our servers, and the Tapora team signs in with mandatory two-factor authentication.

Frequently asked questions

What does the 25-card minimum apply to?+

Per design. Each design needs at least 25 cards: a business that wants 100 cards can have up to 4 different designs (4 × 25). The destination, on the other hand, is chosen card by card, with no minimum: two cards with the same design can open two different pages. For just a few cards, individual cards are $35 each, with no minimum.

Can one order have several designs?+

Yes, as many as you like, as long as each design has at least 25 cards: for example 100 cards for the rooms and 25 for the restaurant and front desk, so 2 designs. You approve each mockup before production.

How do you change where a card leads?+

You write to us and it’s done, without touching the card: the chip points to a Tapora address and we handle the redirect. Batch changes, scheduled destinations and self-service are coming soon.

What does “Coming soon” mean?+

These features are being built at Tapora. They aren’t sold yet: they’ll switch to “Available” on this page the day they really work. Only “Available” features are part of an order.

NFC or QR code?+

Both, on the same card. Guests tap their phone (every iPhone since the XR and the vast majority of Android phones) or scan the code with the camera. No app to install.

What data do you keep about my customers?+

Stats are always included free, for every Business order and for every individual card. They keep no IP address and no identifier: only the time of the tap, the channel (NFC or QR code) and the device type. In line with Québec’s Law 25, nothing is sold or shared.

In which languages?+

Cards: French first in Québec, English first elsewhere. Menus and service pages are shown in 10 languages, chosen by the guest.

How long does it take?+

Longer than a standard card: the mockup to approve, then custom production. The exact date is confirmed with the mockup.

How will payments work?+

Through Stripe, on your business’s own account: the money goes straight to you, Stripe takes its per-transaction fee, and Tapora never sees card numbers. Payments are being built (“Coming soon”).

Tell us what your cards should open.

How many spots, what each one should open, your colours: we come back quickly with a mockup and a price.

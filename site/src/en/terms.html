Who we are

Tapora Cards is operated by Tapora S.E.N.C. (NEQ 3382462367), a Québec business. Questions: contact@taporacards.com.

Pricing

In Canadian dollars: the Québec Card is $35 per card, with no minimum. The Business offer is $30 per card, from 25 cards per order. The exact total is confirmed in writing before any order.

Ordering and payment

Orders are placed by email; for the Business offer, you can also send a request through the form on our site, with no commitment. The payment method and timing, as well as the delivery time, are confirmed in writing with each order.

Business offer (custom design)

Business cards are customized (logo, colours, text, a name for each card), with a minimum of 25 cards per order. We first send you a mock-up: production only starts once you have approved it. Lead time is longer than for a standard card; the planned date is confirmed in writing with the mock-up.

A card installed in Québec features French first, as required by the Charter of the French Language. The “English first” version is reserved for cards installed outside Québec.

What’s included, no subscription

Buying your cards includes, with no subscription or monthly fee, for as long as Tapora operates the service:

  • each card redirecting to the page of your choice, changeable any time without reprogramming the card;
  • your private stats page, counter mode and a notification on every tap;
  • Google review tracking and the monthly email report, if you want it;
  • email support in English and French.

We may improve these features or add new ones. If new options ever became paid, they would stay optional: nothing included with your purchase would become paid.

Fair use

Cards are meant to invite your customers to leave a review or visit the page of your choice. They must not be used to generate automated taps, to buy reviews or trade them for a reward, or for any practice against Google’s rules or the law. In case of abuse, we may suspend the redirect of the card concerned, after notifying you.

A card that doesn’t work

A card that doesn’t work when you receive it is replaced free of charge. Email us a photo of the card.

If the service ever stopped

If Tapora ever stopped operating the service, we would notify you by email at least 90 days in advance.

Your data and your customers’ data

See our privacy policy: no IP address, no lasting cookie and no location is kept when a card is tapped.

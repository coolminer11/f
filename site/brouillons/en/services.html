Restaurants

A living menu, in every guest’s language.

No more laminated menus to reprint. Your dishes, prices and photos are always up to date, and tourists read the menu in their own language.

  • Up to 10 languages — French and English, then Spanish, Chinese and more. The phone’s language is suggested to the guest.
  • Allergens and filters — Health Canada’s priority allergens on every dish, plus vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free filters.
  • The daily special in 10 seconds — and a dish marked “sold out” in one move, mid-service.
  • “Call the server” and “The bill” — no hand-waving, no waiting for someone to walk by.
  • Ordering from the table, if you want it — the guest picks, the kitchen gets the ticket, and payment happens as usual, with the bill.

Hotels

The room concierge, right on your guests’ phones.

The printed binder in the room, only better: always up to date, translated, and connected to your team. Guests have everything at hand — and the front desk gets a breather.

  • One-tap requests — towels, housekeeping, pillows, late checkout: the request arrives with the room number.
  • The concierge — breakfast hours, spa, parking, transport, your favourite spots, useful numbers.
  • Room service — an up-to-date menu, and orders charged to the room if you turn it on.
  • Wi-Fi without asking — the network and password, copied in one tap.

Your questions

Do my guests need to install an app?+

No. They tap the card on their table or in their room with their phone, or scan the code with the camera: the page opens in the browser, with no app, no account and no password.

Does it work with every phone?+

Yes. Every iPhone since the XR and the vast majority of Android phones read the NFC card without an app. For the others, the QR code printed on the table tent opens with any phone’s camera.

Which languages can the menu show?+

Up to ten: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Arabic. In Québec, French comes first, as the Charter of the French Language requires; guests pick their own, and their phone’s language is suggested.

How do Google reviews work?+

Right after their request, guests are asked how their visit is going. The Google link is offered to every guest, whatever their rating. Guests can also write a private note to the team, who reads it in their portal.

What do you do with my guests’ data?+

The page never asks for a name, email, phone number or payment card. We keep the table or room, the language, requests and reviews, for you and your statistics, plus an encrypted fingerprint of the IP address (never the address itself), erased after 30 days, to curb abuse. Nothing is sold or used for advertising, and the data is hosted in Canada. All the details: our privacy rules (in French).

Can guests order?+

Yes, if you want: ordering from the table, or room service at a hotel. The kitchen gets the ticket in the portal, and payment works as usual — with the bill, or charged to the room.

Can I edit my menu myself?+

Yes. Manager mode, protected by a password, lets you change your dishes, prices, photos, announcements and Wi-Fi in seconds. The daily special takes ten seconds from the staff portal.

What do I need to get started?+

Nothing. Tapora builds your menu, prepares the cards and table tents, and trains your team in ten minutes. Staff use the phone or tablet they already have.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Monthly, you can stop whenever you like. Yearly, the service runs to the end of the paid year.

Want to see it?

Fifteen minutes, at your place or on video: you tap the card, the team gets the request, and you see a real month of statistics. In English or French, and fast.
